# Remote AI Engineer Opportunities Brief

## Market Snapshot
The demand for AI Engineers continues to grow as companies increasingly rely on artificial intelligence to enhance their products and services. Remote work has become a standard offering, allowing companies to tap into a global talent pool.

## Representative Openings
- Crossover is looking for AI Engineers with experience in AI and Machine Learning, offering a competitive salary.
- ZipRecruiter lists various roles with flexible hourly rates, catering to different skill levels.
- RemoteRocketship is seeking a Senior AI Engineer with a focus on observability solutions.

## Required Skills & Tech Stack
- Proficiency in AI and Machine Learning frameworks.
- Software development experience, particularly in Python.
- Familiarity with data science and AI model development.

## Compensation & Location Notes
- Salaries range from $43/hr to $200,000/year depending on the role and company.
- Most positions are fully remote, with some companies open to international candidates and offering visa sponsorship.

## Application Strategy
- Direct applications through company websites are recommended.
- Setting job alerts on platforms like LinkedIn can help in finding new openings quickly.
