## Required Skills & Tech Stack
- Proficiency in AI and Machine Learning algorithms  
- Experience with programming languages such as Python, R, or Java  
- Familiarity with AI frameworks like TensorFlow, PyTorch, or Keras  
- Knowledge of data processing and analysis tools  
- Strong problem-solving skills and analytical thinking  

## Compensation & Location Notes
- Compensation for remote AI Engineer roles varies widely, typically ranging from $100,000 to $220,000 depending on experience and company.  
- Most positions are fully remote, with no specific location constraints, although some may prefer candidates in certain regions (e.g., North Carolina).

## Application Strategy
- Tailor your resume and cover letter to highlight relevant skills and experiences.  
- Utilize networking platforms like LinkedIn to connect with hiring managers and recruiters.  
- Apply through company websites and job boards to increase visibility.
