## Compensation & Location Notes
- **Average Salary**: The average gross salary for an AI Engineer in Brazil is approximately **R$196,971** (around **$38,000**), with entry-level positions starting at **R$140,978** and senior roles reaching up to **R$230,074**.
- **Monthly Salary Range**: Remote AI Engineers can expect to earn between **$4,500** and **$8,300** per month, depending on experience and specialization.

## Application Strategy for Brazilian Candidates
1. **Tailor Your Resume**: Customize your resume for each application, highlighting relevant skills and projects.
2. **Networking**: Engage with professionals in the field through platforms like LinkedIn to discover job opportunities.
3. **Portfolio Development**: Build a portfolio showcasing your projects, particularly those that demonstrate your ability to deploy AI solutions in real-world scenarios.
4. **Quality Over Quantity**: Aim for **15-20 applications per week**, focusing on quality and relevance.
5. **Prepare for Interviews**: Be ready to discuss your technical skills and past projects, and demonstrate your problem-solving abilities in practical scenarios.
