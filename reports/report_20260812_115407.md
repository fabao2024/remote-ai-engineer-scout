# Remote AI Engineer Opportunities Brief — Brazil
## Market Snapshot
Recent job listings for remote AI Engineer positions indicate a growing demand for professionals in Brazil. Companies are increasingly open to hiring talent from Brazil, especially in the AI and machine learning sectors. The trend towards remote work has expanded opportunities for Brazilian candidates, allowing them to apply for roles that were previously limited to specific geographic locations.

## Active Remote Employers
1. **CI&T** - Actively hiring for a Senior AI Engineer position specifically for candidates in Brazil.
2. **Apheris** - Looking for a Principal ML Scientist with a focus on predictive toxicology, open to global candidates.
3. **Arc.dev** - Offers freelance AI roles that are available to candidates worldwide.
4. **UL Solutions** - Hiring for an AI Developer with remote or hybrid options, specifically in São Paulo.
5. **Working Nomads** - A platform listing various remote AI jobs available to candidates globally.

## Representative Openings
- **Senior AI Engineer, Brazil** at CI&T: Full-time, remote, focused on AI and software engineering.
- **Principal ML Scientist** at Apheris: Fully remote, competitive salary, requires advanced ML skills.
- **AI Automation Specialist** at Arc.dev: Freelance, part-time, requires AI and Python skills.
- **AI Developer** at UL Solutions: Remote or hybrid, senior level, focused on data platforms.
- **Remote AI Jobs** on Working Nomads: Various roles available, full-time and freelance.

## Required Skills & Tech Stack
- **Core Skills**: AI, machine learning, Python, data platforms, software engineering.
- **Tools**: PyTorch, APIs, various data science tools.
- **Experience**: Ranges from mid-level to senior positions, with some roles requiring advanced degrees (PhD or MSc).

## Compensation & Location Notes
- Compensation varies by role; some companies do not specify salary ranges.
- Most roles are remote-friendly, with specific positions open to candidates in Brazil.
- Time-zone compatibility is generally flexible, but some roles may require overlap with US time zones.

## Application Strategy for Brazilian Candidates
- Highlight relevant experience in AI and machine learning in your resume.
- Network through platforms like LinkedIn and industry-specific forums to connect with hiring managers.
- Tailor your application to emphasize your ability to work in a remote setting and your familiarity with global teams.
